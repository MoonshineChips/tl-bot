# TL Bot Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-10-24*

TL Bot is the community bot of the **TL Pro** Discord server and the official TL Pro Telegram chat, operated by the
TL team ([terlauncher.com](https://terlauncher.com)). This policy explains what data the bot processes.

## What we collect

- Discord and Telegram user IDs and display names.
- Activity counters used for levels and achievements: number of messages, active days, voice minutes.
  **Message text is not stored.**
- XP, coins, achievements and reputation records (who thanked whom, message ID, time).
- Support ticket metadata: ticket number, category, timestamps. Messages you write in a ticket stay in the
  Discord thread or Telegram chat; the bot does not keep their text.
- If you link your Discord and Telegram accounts, the link between the two IDs.

## What we do not collect

Passwords, payment data, presence/status, message content outside our announcements channel. An e-mail address is
processed only if you type it into a closed beta application yourself; the team deletes it after checking the
purchase.

## Why

To run levels, achievements, reputation, support tickets, closed beta applications and anti-spam on the TL Pro
Discord server and Telegram chat, and to mirror TL announcements to our Telegram channel.

## Where and how long

On our own server, accessible only to the TL team, for as long as these features are active.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share this data with third parties.

## Your choices

You can ask us to delete your data by opening a ticket (the ticket button on Discord or `/ticket` in the Telegram
bot [@TerlauncherBot](https://t.me/TerlauncherBot)). We delete it within 30 days.

## Contact

TL team — via a ticket on the TL Pro Discord server or the Telegram bot [@TerlauncherBot](https://t.me/TerlauncherBot).
